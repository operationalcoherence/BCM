## A. Core theory — Vigneaux (2026), JEM 24(7)
| Concept | Definition (BCM usage) | In BCM |
|---|---|---|
| Autopoiesis | A living system is a network that produces the components that reproduce that network and its boundary (Maturana & Varela) | Grounds the reflexive loop: action changes the system that produced it |
| Concrete networks / abstract behaviors | Biological activity and observed behavior are separate domains: neither can be deduced from the other | Notes record behavior/language, never "the person inside" |
| Closed nervous system / perturbation | Stimuli perturb but do not instruct; the system's own structure decides its response | Perturbation audit; outreach can't dictate change, only invite it |
| Emotion as disposition for action | Emotions and moods are bodily dispositions that set which actions are possible right now; a change of emotion changes how one senses, reasons and acts | Why emotion is the cornerstone of the terrain |
| Five emotions | Acceptance, indifference, distrust, aggression, defiance, in descending potential to take up a new idea | Emotion model, colors, terrain heights |
| Indifference as transition | Most movement between emotions passes through indifference; it is precarious (neither for nor against) and also a place to rest | Indifference ridge |
| Energy gradient of transition | Some transitions cost more than others (defiance→acceptance is rare and costly; distrust↔acceptance costs more than indifference→acceptance) | Climb cost, bare rock face, routes |
| No stable emotion | Even acceptance can shift; it needs continual care | Re-synthesis prompts |
| Possibility landscape | A visualization of emotional texture showing which transitions are needed, through which emotions, and where to spend energy (term via Juarrero) | Pattern Synthesis terrain as a whole |
| Emotions-first strategy | Connect people to acceptance (or something close to it), then link acceptance to the desired behavior; for acceptance, indifference and transitional zones | Taking Action pathways; popover |
| Behaviors-first strategy | Introduce the behavior without its label, fitted to the existing severe emotion, and let a small emotional shift follow; for aggression, distrust, defiance | Taking Action; PS explainer (task C3) |
| Sensorimotor schemes | Lasting, ordered bundles of sensing-and-moving patterns; people meet situations through schemes they already have | Habits as context; "people are already doing something" |
| Habit precarity | Habits are self-maintaining schemes that fade if unused | Why entrenched patterns persist (hysteresis) |
| Environment strategy | Change the environment people can observe rather than asking them to change; their reference point may shift slowly | Act-anywhere actions; constraint reconfiguration |
| Language strategy | Language brings forth the world it speaks of (Winograd & Flores); change shared vocabulary to change the cultural landscape | Lexical world-making; fire sub-type reframing |
| Perceiver-dependent reality | What counts as a relevant world depends on the perceiver's structure (Varela) | Voices read the same word differently |
| Starratt painting | A commissioned town map of emotional reactions; colors blend or break at transitions, with splatters of other emotions inside regions | Color palette; terrain blending; "color without terrain" |

## B. Review-introduced concepts
| Concept | Definition | Source | In BCM |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grasping / designing | Q.1–Q.4 grasp the context; Q.5–Q.7 design for it; one cascading flow | Review | Page framing, gap analysis |
| Thread | Graph edge linking notes/answers by shared specific tokens or vector proximity across question boundaries. On screen: "a word or phrase that two or more answers or notes share" | Review; v171 | PAR, PS (task A1) |
| Pattern that runs through it | A two-word phrase in more than one note or answer; drawn as a loop on the 3D map when ticked | App (v171 name) | PS |
| Link | User-made connection from a note to an answer, the goal or another note (supports, contradicts, reinforces), made with + Link on Mapping Landscape | App | MAP, PS (WHAT YOU LINKED) |
| Voice | A perspective/stakeholder group that authored notes | App | Normalization, coworking |
| Indifference ridge | Raised terrain wherever indifferent notes sit, crossing other emotions | Review | PS |
| Bare rock face | Two distant emotions meeting with no indifference between them; a costly climb, treated as a design challenge | Review | PS legend, synthetic bridging |
| Low ground | Distrust (low), aggression (lower), defiance (lowest) | Review | PS legend |
| Background-noise paradox / medium word | A term present everywhere is the medium, not a signal ("water in the fishbowl"). In the engine: in ≥40% of answers and notes and split into 2+ recurring phrases | Review; v172 | Saturation filter, ONE WORD, SEVERAL MEANINGS |
| Same-meaning declaration | The person says two words mean the same; only then does Patterns read them as one (split by default, never merge on its own) | v173 (THE SAME THING?) | Tokenizer |
| Co-occurrence profiling | Meaning comes from a word's neighbors; tag sub-types from a sliding window | Review | E2 |
| Semantic inertia / topological anchoring | Fixed anchors + local relaxation keep the user's spatial memory across edits | Review | F2 |
| Voice normalization | Weight voices so that a small, dense voice isn't flattened by note count | Review | V5 |
| Ecology of action | Once launched, an action escapes its intent and may come back on you; follow it and correct it | Morin (2008, p. 55) | TA watch/stop signals |
| Strategy vs. program | Programs suit stable settings and need no vigilance; strategy is for changing ones | Morin (2008) | TA pathway framing |
| Sense-making in complexity | Different domains (known, knowable, complex, chaos) call for different ways of deciding | Kurtz & Snowden (2003) | TA reference; pathway choice [use TBD] |
| Attractor basin / constraints | Habits and institutions form wells that constraints hold in place; change the constraints, not just the path | Juarrero (2023) | T5 |
| Trajectory optimization vs. constraint reconfiguration | Cheapest path within the rules vs. changing the rules | Review / Juarrero | T5, Q.4–Q.7 |
| Hysteresis | A note's state depends on its history; entrenched opposition costs more to cross | Review | F6 |
| Action friction field | An action's energy cost varies with the terrain it crosses | Review | F3 |
| Act here / act anywhere × easy / difficult | Plots actions by reach (context-specific vs. universal) and by energy | axis.png | T4 |
| Synthesis→Action handoff | Rule that turns a terrain vector into sequenced moves | Review | T6 |
| Reflexive (autopoietic) loop | Acting changes the terrain, so map → act → re-map | Review; Morin, Varela | F4 |
| Relational meaning (sense-making) | Value is not in the thing but in how an organism couples with it (sucrose is a nutrient only to the cell that uses it) | Thompson (2007) | "Where it is easiest to get to"; meaning is relational |
| Lexical world-making | Track and prompt reframing of generic terms | Review / Vigneaux | F7 |
| Perturbation audit | Treat incoming notes as perturbations to test, not instructions | Review / Vigneaux | F7 |
