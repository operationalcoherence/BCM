Item 3 in his order of the second generation of insight types (BCM_TASK_QUEUE.md, 11-20 20:06), after v276's Groups Across Ground. His call: "do it", 25 Nov 2026 15:32. Patch: tools/patches/v277_risk_after_the_goal.py.

## What it does
- **Pattern Analysis:** a card, RISK AFTER THE GOAL, under WHY IT BEHAVES THIS WAY, after WHO STANDS WHERE. It reads the low ground close to the goal (the last third of the way, on the side that hinders, on distrust, aggression, or defiance): where the change would already count as done and can still be undone. Each emotion says how: defiance keeps the old way going under the new name, aggression pushes the change back, distrust holds back or leaves (Vigneaux, 2026). It then says whether what you would do (Q.5–Q.7) reaches it, any weight you put on it yourself ("the most dangerous", "the biggest risk", "critical"), and one implication: decide early what the goal looks like in how people work.
- **Taking Action:** a row in THE TEST, AFTER THE GOAL, with YES / PARTLY / NOT YET.
- **The map:** on YOUR LANDSCAPE, a numbered hollow at the goal on the side that hinders is titled "Where it can come undone at the goal".
- **Say once:** when the card shows, the CLOSE TO THE GOAL chapter drops its closing line about judging the change by what people do.

## The reach rule (one rule, both pages)
`gsmAfterReach()`: a design answer or note (Q.5–Q.7) sharing a word with the note (the words NOTHING YOU WOULD DO ANSWERS THESE YET reads), or a link drawn. A "No" to DOES THIS ANSWER IT? overrides. A note that itself hinders on a low emotion never counts as reaching it, even if it is filed under Q.5–Q.7. This is word evidence, so when everything is reached, the card says to check that it is reached in practice.

## Inactive unless it applies
Line maps only. When the map is big enough for its own low grounds (gsmSystems, 12+ notes), only the notes of a low ground centred close to the goal count. On a small map, two such notes are enough.
