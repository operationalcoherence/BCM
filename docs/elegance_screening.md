# Preliminary screening: what the reading pages could do without

Written 2026-11-08. All wording and every judgment here is Claude's, made by
reading the three reading pages rendered from Gregory's real workbook at v101
with every fold open. **This is a first pass for him to rule on, not a decision
and not a work order.** Nothing here has been removed.

The standard applied is the one he set on 2026-11-08: power and simplicity —
offer the core findings and nothing else. The question asked of every section
was *does this say something he can act on that nothing else on the page
already says?*

Confidence is marked. **Safe** means the content is duplicated somewhere else,
so removing it loses nothing. **Judgment** means it is a real observation and
the argument against it is that it is weak, which is his call, not Claude's.

## 2. The "meaning of the meaning" blocks — judgment, but a strong case

Three places restate figures the reader was given moments earlier, under a
heading announcing a deeper reading:

- Synthesis, ABOUT WHAT YOU COLLECTED: "Balanced... Spread out... Grounded
  across the board..." after the counts that produced them.
- Synthesis, WHAT IT MIGHT MEAN: "Heavy on Q.5–Q.7. 68 groups... Concentrated.
  'fire' is the most repeated (19)... Contested ground. 19 groups... Support
  not yet used. 8 groups..." Every one of those numbers appears in the bucket
  list directly above it.
- In Parallel: "The meaning of that meaning."

These are the clearest case of a less-than-powerful feature in the passage's
sense: they add a layer without adding a finding. Cutting all three is the
single largest word saving available that costs no information.

## 3. Word-spotting, not pattern-finding — judgment

These report that a common English word is present. They do not read the
person's subject.

| Section | What it says on his workbook | Note |

| SOUNDS LIKE A CONDITION | Q.1, Q.4, Q.5, Q.6 and 4 more use "must", "need", "needs" | Fires on 8 of 8 pieces. A signal present everywhere distinguishes nothing. |

Worth noting against cutting them: they are cheap, and on a thinner workbook
they may be among the few things the engine can say at all. A ranking that
demotes a signal firing on nearly everything would handle these without a
deletion — which is an argument for letting Layer 1 decide rather than cutting
now.

## 6. What should not be touched

Named so the screening is not read as a licence to cut broadly. These are the
method working:

- In Parallel's TWO PEOPLE TWO SIDES, PLACED ONE WAY TIED THE OTHER and THE
  EMOTION CHANGES. These are the strongest findings in the method and the
  closest to the "surprisingly deep insights" he asked for.
- Synthesis's bucket list in WHAT IT MIGHT MEAN — it is where both sections he
  named ("patterns that reach the goal", "patterns needed to reach the goal")
  already live.
- WHAT IT RESTS ON on both pages. Grounding is not a finding, but it is what
  keeps the method honest about how much to trust the rest.
- A NAME THAT RECURS. Weak today at three names, and the only place
  capitalized names surface at all. Re-judge after item 2, not before.
- Everything in AT A GLANCE.

## Rough size of the cut

Not measured by building it, only estimated from the rendered text: sections 1,
2 and 5 together are on the order of 400–500 words across the three pages, and
cost no finding. Sections 3 and 4 are perhaps another 400, and those are
judgment calls.

Against pages of 1,860, 1,957 and 1,013 words, that is a meaningful share of
the way to one screen before a single ranking decision is made.

## How this should be used

Gregory goes through it and rules. Where he does not rule, the default stays
what it is today — nothing is cut without him saying so. The safe duplicates in
section 1 are the only ones Claude would act on with a simple yes.
