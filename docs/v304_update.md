# v304: the result looks the same, not the place

His note on Broadview's story (26 Nov 2026 10:18, approved option 2 at 10:24): a home that did the work does look different; what looks the same before a fire is the result. The one visible difference is the change to the place, which is itself a reason people hold back.

Place-map story, "What keeps recreating it" (the risk reading), for every workbook:

- old: "Until something goes wrong, the place that did nothing looks no different from the one that did the work, so every season without a fire confirms the choice to wait."
- new, homes and fire: "Until a fire comes, the work shows only as a change to the place, never as a house saved, so every season without a fire confirms the choice to wait."
- new, otherwise: "Until something goes wrong, the work shows only as a change to the place, never as a loss avoided, so every year that nothing goes wrong confirms the choice to wait."
