# v313: F8 step 6, the dashboard reading

Lead a project → open a project: under EVERY WORKBOOK, **READING THEM TOGETHER**.

- "Patterns suggests; you decide." said once, at the top.
- Voice filter: Every voice, or one voice. Everything below follows it.
- **See everyone's ground together →** opens one combined copy of everyone's answers and notes (only the chosen voice, if one is picked). Pattern Analysis shows the ground they make together on the 3D relief, and Taking Action reads all of it. The lead's own workbook is parked, like with an invite link, and comes back with Back to my own workbook. The copy is never sent and is dropped on the way back. Place-map projects use the lead's place.
- **WHERE THEY SPLIT:** the same thing (a two- or three-word phrase two or more people use) put on the help side by one person and the hinder side by another, or tied to acceptance by one and to distrust, aggression, or defiance by another. Each with both people's own sentences and where they are.
- **WHERE THEY AGREE:** the same thing on the same side, in the same question, or with the same emotion, by two or more people. Phrases from the shared goal are left out here.
- **EMOTION BY VOICE:** a bar per voice, with the shares, and one line on whether the voices lean different ways.
- **WHAT NOBODY HAS SAID YET:** questions nobody answered; the lead's voices nobody has answered as; people with no notes; an empty help or hinder side; emotions nobody marked; no note marked as seen; mostly assumed sources.
- Single words are never stated on their own here; only phrases.

Worked out on the lead's device from the workbooks already read for the table: no extra database reads.
