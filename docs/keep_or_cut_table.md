## What the split says

Ten candidates on Analysis, five on Synthesis, and they fail differently.
Analysis's problem is weak findings — word-spotting and lookup tables.
Synthesis's problem is restatement — saying a number again under a new
heading. Those want different remedies: Analysis wants cutting, Synthesis
wants consolidating.

In Parallel is not covered here. Its candidates are in `elegance_screening.md`:
"The meaning of that meaning", and the overlap between "Where to start" and
Synthesis's "Where to look first".
