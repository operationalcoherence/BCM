## What it does
v276's WHO STANDS WHERE already makes this reading on Pattern Analysis: what you would do turns on a group, and distrust is aimed at that same group where things hinder. v278 carries it onto the cards. A card holding the notes that make the reading (the design that turns on the group, or the distrust aimed at it) gets a new first move, which also shows as FIRST MOVE in the card's strip:

> Settle who designs it first: the plan turns on [group], and your notes distrust [it] ([notes]). Design it with people who doubt the change, and let [group] go first in doing what [it asks] of others.

The card's other moves follow unchanged. `gsmStructSum.groups[].ob` keeps what made the reading (`d` the design refs, `td` the distrust refs).

## Inactive unless it applies
Only when WHO STANDS WHERE makes the "relies on the people it distrusts" reading, and only on cards holding those notes.
