# v181 (preview only) — 2026-11-17

Priority 2, T6: the handoff from Synthesis to Action. All wording is Claude's.

Every strategy card with two or more ways now has **MOVES, IN ORDER** in THE WAYS FORWARD, SKETCHED ON THE GROUND. It is built from the ground and the action grid:
1. **Start** with the card's easy actions that act anywhere (the action grid on In Parallel). Failing that, its easy actions that act here; failing that, the acceptance notes on the easy way. Small and visible.
2. **Next**, the indifference notes the ways pass: win them over before they tip.
3. **Then** the harder way to the resistance it meets, behaviors first (Vigneaux, 2026), with the card's difficult actions last.
4. **After each move**, update the notes passed and read the ground again (ties to When to stop and readjust).

On his good fire card: start with N4 and N15; next N31; then way A to N9, N29 and N30.

The action grid's points are shared as `window.gsmGridPts` (In Parallel builds them; Taking Action reads them).
