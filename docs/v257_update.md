## What changed (every workbook with several stories)
- Six paragraphs become four (about 650 words to about 370 on the 11-20 workbook).
  1. The pattern and how it moves (the old paragraph 5, which repeated it, is folded in as one sentence).
  2. What keeps it going. If Q.1 names a lack of knowledge, it now says so ("A lack of knowledge is among the reasons you gave, but the notes point more strongly elsewhere") instead of "What produces this is not a lack of information."
  3. What it protects and why that is reasonable, condensed to a few sentences.
  4. The ending is no longer a general principle. It says what would have to be provided another way, which of the user's own answers already reach it, and what nothing they would design yet answers.
- The levers are matched from the user's own Q.5 to Q.7 answers and notes (Q.2 too, for keeping the familiar order): keeping the order people rely on, changing what people are expected and rewarded for, measuring what people do rather than what is declared, engaging those who resist. Each one shows only when its mechanism is present; a missing one is named as not yet answered.
- "Who belongs" is gone (weakly grounded), here and in AT A GLANCE; "each person's place" stands in only when the workbook has the partway chapter.
- Matches the approved definitions: legitimacy language stays tied to acceptance of the present arrangement; nothing ties aggression, distrust, defiance, or indifference to legitimacy; the middle reads as "neither for nor against" (indifference).
