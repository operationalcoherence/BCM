On a place map only (a place-only workbook, and YOUR PLACE IN 3D), both tours now read the place. The line map's tours and every page text are unchanged.

1. **Opening (both tours), READING THE MAP · How to read this map:** "The land is the real place. Its hills and valleys are the terrain itself, not your notes. Each color is an emotion your notes carry, painted around where you placed them. Where there is no color, no notes are nearby, so there is no information there."
2. **One stop per emotion on the map**, in the order acceptance, indifference, distrust, aggression, defiance. Each is titled "Where [emotion] gathers", names its notes, says whether they are close together or spread across the place, and gives what the emotion means. The meanings follow his confirmed ones: acceptance is where the goal already has people aligned with it; distrust, aggression, and defiance are not bad in themselves; the road back to acceptance grows longer from each, and is longest from defiance.
3. **Where distant emotions sit closest:** the nearest pair of notes two or more steps apart (neither one indifference). It says whether an indifference note lies between them ("the likeliest way across") or not ("takes the most energy, in either direction").
4. **What the goal sits among:** the note nearest the goal and its emotion.
5. **Notes with no emotion yet:** a count, when there are any (the white pins).
6. **What a move costs:** the same as the line map's, but "Climbing takes far more than falling" becomes "Moving toward acceptance takes far more than moving away from it". On real land, "climbing" reads as walking uphill.
7. **Synthesis acts** on a place: WHERE ACCEPTANCE GATHERS, WHERE DISTRUST, AGGRESSION, AND DEFIANCE GATHER, WHERE INDIFFERENCE GATHERS, and HOW THEY MEET ON THE LAND, each opened by one plain line.
