# v330 · WHERE THEY AGREE AND SPLIT as a bar chart

Gregory, 27 Nov 17:22: "this should be a graphic"; "a conventional pie chart or bar graph is fine". On Project at a glance, WHERE THEY SPLIT and WHERE THEY AGREE are one horizontal bar chart: one bar per thing two or more people name (up to 6 splits, then up to 6 agreements); length = people, colours = the side of the line each person put it on (blue helps, orange hinders, grey not placed); a SPLIT or AGREE label at the right. Tap a bar: the old sentence and their words open under it. Phones: label above the bar.
